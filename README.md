# 🛠️ BlueHire

## 📖 Overview

**BlueHire** is a location-based web application dedicated to empowering blue-collar workers. The platform serves as a bridge, seamlessly connecting skilled workers with clients in their local vicinity who need their services. 

By focusing on proximity and ease of use, BlueHire eliminates the friction of finding reliable local help for clients, while providing a dedicated, accessible space for blue-collar professionals to grow their client base and manage their work.

---

## ✨ Core Features

* **📍 Location-Based Matching:** Utilizes geolocation to connect clients with the nearest available workers, ensuring quick response times and local community support.
* **📅 Streamlined Booking Functionality:** Allows clients to easily schedule services, while giving workers a clear, organized view of their upcoming jobs and availability.
* **⭐ User Reviews & Ratings:** A transparent feedback system where clients can leave reviews after a completed job, helping workers build a reputable profile and giving future clients peace of mind.
* **👷 Dedicated Profiles:** Separate, tailored profile dashboards for both Workers (showcasing skills, rates, and reviews) and Clients (managing job requests and history).
* **💬 Direct Communication:** In-app messaging to negotiate details, clarify job requirements, and keep all work-related communication in one place.
